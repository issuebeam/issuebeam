## LinkedIn - Post professionale
*Più incentrato sulla produttività del team e la gestione del debito tecnico.*

**Testo:**
The rise of AI coding assistants (like Cursor and GitHub Copilot) has massively accelerated development speed. But I noticed a structural problem in this new "vibe coding" era: project management and technical debt tracking are breaking down.

Developers mention bugs or future tasks inside their AI chats, and when the chat is closed, that context is lost forever.

To solve this, I built and open-sourced **issuebeam**. 

It’s a lightweight, cross-platform bridge that allows AI agents to directly list, create, and close official GitHub Issues right from the IDE terminal. It requires zero third-party dependencies, meaning any AI can execute it effortlessly across any OS.

If you lead an engineering team or use AI to code, this ensures your issue tracker stays in sync with your AI interactions. 

It’s MIT Licensed and free. I'd love to hear your thoughts on how we can better integrate AI agents with our traditional project management workflows!

Link to repo: https://github.com/issuebeam/issuebeam
